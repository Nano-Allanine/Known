# KNOWN — System Purpose

## 1. Introduction

KNOWN is a digital identity verification platform designed to help individuals and organizations establish, manage, and verify digital identities through structured identity information, supporting documents, and facial verification.

The system provides a centralized platform where identity information can be securely managed and where authorized users can request and perform identity verification.

## 2. Purpose of the System

The primary purpose of KNOWN is to provide a secure, reliable, and traceable digital process for verifying that an individual is who they claim to be.

The system aims to reduce reliance on manual identity verification processes, improve the accuracy of identity verification, reduce identity-related fraud, and provide organizations with a structured way to manage verification requests and results.

## 3. Key Objectives

KNOWN aims to:

* Create and manage digital identity records.
* Allow individuals to provide and maintain identity information.
* Manage identity documents and supporting evidence.
* Allow authorized organizations or verifiers to request identity verification.
* Support facial verification as an identity verification method.
* Record and manage verification results.
* Maintain a history of verification activities.
* Provide audit logs for important system activities.
* Protect identity information through appropriate authentication and access controls.
* Provide a foundation that can support additional verification methods and integrations in future versions.

## 4. Target Environment

KNOWN is intended to operate as a web-based platform accessible through modern web browsers on computers, tablets, and smartphones.

The initial version will use a React frontend, Django REST Framework backend, and PostgreSQL database.

## 5. Version 1 Focus

KNOWN Version 1 will focus on:

1. User authentication and role management.
2. Digital identity management.
3. Identity document management.
4. Verification requests.
5. Verification results and history.
6. Facial verification.
7. Audit logging.
8. Administrative and verification dashboards.

Advanced biometric technologies such as DNA verification, fingerprint hardware, and iris scanning are outside the scope of Version 1.

## 6. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a functional digital identity verification platform that demonstrates how individuals and organizations can securely manage identity information and conduct traceable identity verification through a centralized system.

KNOWN is designed to provide a foundation that can later be expanded with additional biometric technologies, external identity providers, organizational integrations, and other advanced verification services.
