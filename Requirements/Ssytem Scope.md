Purpose

KNOWN is a digital identity and verification platform designed to allow individuals to establish a digital identity and enable authorized users to securely verify that identity.

The system will combine conventional identity information with facial verification and QR-based verification.

In Scope — V1

KNOWN will include:

Account registration
User account creation
Secure authentication
Digital identity enrollment
Collection of approved identity information
Generation of a unique KNOWN ID
Identity status management
Digital identity profile

Apply appropriate biometric security controls
